<div class="mw-heading mw-heading5"><h5 id="Usage_notes">Usage notes</h5><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%AA%E3%82%8B&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Usage notes"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<ul><li>This term is often spelled in <span class="Latn" lang="en"><a href="/wiki/kana#English" title="kana">kana</a></span>.</li>
<li>Sense "to become":
<ul><li>When a noun or an adjectival noun (na-adjective) precedes <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">なる</span> it is followed by <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AB#Japanese" title="に">に</a></span>.
<dl><dd><span class="h-usage-example"><i class="Jpan mention e-example" lang="ja"><ruby>車<rp>(</rp><rt>くるま</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>に<b>なる</b></i> ― <i class="e-transliteration tr">kuruma ni <b>naru</b></i> ― <span class="e-translation"><b>become</b> a car</span></span></dd>
<dd><span class="h-usage-example"><i class="Jpan mention e-example" lang="ja"><ruby>綺<rp>(</rp><rt>き</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>麗<rp>(</rp><rt>れい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>に<b>なる</b></i> ― <i class="e-transliteration tr">kirei ni <b>naru</b></i> ― <span class="e-translation"><b>become</b> beautiful</span></span></dd></dl></li>
<li>When an adjective (i-adjective) precedes <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">なる</span> it appears in its <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">-<a href="/wiki/%E3%81%8F#Japanese" title="く">く</a></span> form.
<dl><dd><span class="h-usage-example"><i class="Jpan mention e-example" lang="ja"><ruby>優<rp>(</rp><rt>やさ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>しく<b>なる</b></i> ― <i class="e-transliteration tr">yasashiku <b>naru</b></i> ― <span class="e-translation"><b>become</b> kind</span></span></dd></dl></li>
<li>The causative is suppleted by <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%99%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="する">する</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>suru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.
<dl><dd><span class="h-usage-example"><i class="Jpan mention e-example" lang="ja"><ruby>部<rp>(</rp><rt>へ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>屋<rp>(</rp><rt>や</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>がきれいに<b>なる</b></i> ― <i class="e-transliteration tr">heya ga kirei ni <b>naru</b></i> ― <span class="e-translation">The room <b>becomes</b> tidy.</span></span></dd>
<dd><span class="h-usage-example"><i class="Jpan mention e-example" lang="ja"><ruby>私<rp>(</rp><rt>わたし</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>が<ruby>部<rp>(</rp><rt>へ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>屋<rp>(</rp><rt>や</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>をきれいに<b>する</b></i> ― <i class="e-transliteration tr">watashi ga heya o kirei ni <b>suru</b></i> ― <span class="e-translation">I <b>make</b> the room tidy.</span></span></dd></dl></li></ul></li></ul>
