<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Etymology">Etymology</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E9%AB%98%E3%81%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Etymology"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<p>From <span class="etyl"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Middle_Japanese" class="extiw" title="w:Early Middle Japanese">Early Middle Japanese</a></span> adjective <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E9%AB%98%E3%81%97&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="高し (page does not exist)"><ruby>高<rp>(</rp><rt>たか</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>し</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>takashi</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, with the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E7%B5%82%E6%AD%A2%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="終止形"><ruby>終<rp>(</rp><rt>しゅう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>止<rp>(</rp><rt>し</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>shūshikei</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/predicative" title="predicative">predicative</a> form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> ending in <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%97#Japanese" title="し">し</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>shi</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> first replaced with the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E9%80%A3%E4%BD%93%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="連体形"><ruby>連<rp>(</rp><rt>れん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>体<rp>(</rp><rt>たい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>rentaikei</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/attributive" title="attributive">attributive</a> form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> ending in <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a title="い">い</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>i</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>. From <span class="etyl"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Japanese" class="extiw" title="w:Old Japanese">Old Japanese</a></span>, from <span class="etyl"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Japonic_language" class="extiw" title="w:Proto-Japonic language">Proto-Japonic</a></span> <i class="Latn mention" lang="jpx-pro"><a href="/wiki/Reconstruction:Proto-Japonic/taka" class="mw-redirect" title="Reconstruction:Proto-Japonic/taka">&#42;taka</a></i>.
