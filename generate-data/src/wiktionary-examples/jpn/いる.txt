<div class="mw-heading mw-heading5"><h5 id="Usage_notes">Usage notes</h5><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%84%E3%82%8B&amp;action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Usage notes"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<ul><li>The <a href="/wiki/Appendix:Japanese_glossary#ren&#39;yōkei" title="Appendix:Japanese glossary">ren'yōkei</a> of this verb, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%84#Japanese" title="い">い</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>i</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, cannot be used conjunctively (see <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E4%B8%AD%E6%AD%A2%E6%B3%95&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="中止法 (page does not exist)"><ruby>中<rp>(</rp><rt>ちゅう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>止<rp>(</rp><rt>し</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>法<rp>(</rp><rt>ほう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>chūshihō</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">conjunctive usage</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>); instead <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%8A%E3%82%8A#Japanese" title="おり">おり</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>ori</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> (from <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%8A%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="おる">おる</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>oru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>) is preferred. Put another way, the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E4%B8%AD%E6%AD%A2%E5%BD%A2&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="中止形 (page does not exist)"><ruby>中<rp>(</rp><rt>ちゅう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>止<rp>(</rp><rt>し</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>chūshikei</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">conjunctive usage form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> of <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">いる</strong></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>iru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> is <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%8A%E3%82%8A#Japanese" title="おり">おり</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>ori</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>. The negatives <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%84%E3%81%AA%E3%81%8F&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="いなく (page does not exist)">いなく</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>inaku</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%84%E3%81%9A#Japanese" title="いず">いず</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>izu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> are likewise avoided and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%8A%E3%82%89%E3%81%9A&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="おらず (page does not exist)">おらず</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>orazu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> preferred.</li>
<li>(<a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Japanese:_teiru">etymology 1 sense&#160;3</a>): Colloquially the initial <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">い</span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>i</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> is often omitted, resulting in forms such as
<dl><dd><span class="Jpan" lang="ja">〜ている</span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>~te iru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> → <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">〜てる</span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>~te ru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>;</dd>
