<div class="mw-heading mw-heading5"><h5 id="Usage_notes">Usage notes</h5><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%AA%E3%81%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=10" title="Edit section: Usage notes"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<ul><li>This term is often spelled in <span class="Latn" lang="en"><a href="/wiki/kana#English" title="kana">kana</a></span>.</li>
<li>In public speech and written language, the irregular expressions <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%9B%E3%82%93#Japanese" title="ありません">ありません</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>arimasen</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%82%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%9B%E3%82%93%E3%81%A7%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="ありませんでした (page does not exist)">ありませんでした</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>arimasen deshita</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> (past) are commonly prescribed instead of <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">ない</strong><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7%E3%81%99#Japanese" title="です">です</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai desu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AA%E3%81%8B%E3%81%A3%E3%81%9F#Japanese" title="なかった">なかった</a><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7%E3%81%99#Japanese" title="です">です</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nakatta desu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.</li>
<li>When used as an auxiliary adjective (<a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Japanese:_auxiliary">etymology 2, adjective sense&#160;8</a>), it follows:
<ol><li>the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E9%80%A3%E7%94%A8%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="連用形"><ruby>連<rp>(</rp><rt>れん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>用<rp>(</rp><rt>よう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i><a href="/wiki/Appendix:Japanese_glossary#ren&#39;yōkei" title="Appendix:Japanese glossary">ren'yōkei</a></i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/stem" title="stem">stem</a> or <a href="/wiki/continuative" title="continuative">continuative</a> form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> of <a href="/wiki/%E3%81%84-adjective" title="い-adjective"><i>-i</i> adjectives</a>, which ends in <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%8F#Japanese" title="く">く</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>-ku</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>;
<dl><dd><span lang="ja" class="Jpan e-example"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%93%E3%81%AE#Japanese" title="この">この</a><a href="/wiki/%E3%83%91%E3%82%BD%E3%82%B3%E3%83%B3#Japanese" title="パソコン">パソコン</a>は<a href="/wiki/%E5%AE%89%E3%81%84#Japanese" title="安い"><ruby>安<rp>(</rp><rt>やす</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>く</a><b>ない</b>。</span><dl><dd><i><span class="e-transliteration tr">Kono pasokon wa yasuku <b>nai</b>.</span></i></dd><dd><span class="e-translation">This computer is <b>not</b> cheap.</span></dd></dl></dd></dl></li>
<li>and the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E9%80%A3%E7%94%A8%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="連用形"><ruby>連<rp>(</rp><rt>れん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>用<rp>(</rp><rt>よう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i><a href="/wiki/Appendix:Japanese_glossary#ren&#39;yōkei" title="Appendix:Japanese glossary">ren'yōkei</a></i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/stem" title="stem">stem</a> or <a href="/wiki/continuative" title="continuative">continuative</a> form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> of <a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AA-adjective" title="な-adjective"><i>-na</i> adjectives</a> and nominals, which ends in <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7#Japanese" title="で">で</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>de</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.

The resulting <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7%E3%81%AF#Japanese" title="では">では</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>de wa</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> can be contracted to <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%98%E3%82%83#Japanese" title="じゃ">じゃ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>ja</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.</li>

<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Etymology_3">Etymology 3</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%AA%E3%81%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=14" title="Edit section: Etymology 3"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<ul class="etymid" id="Japanese:&#95;negative&#95;suffix" data-lang="ja" data-id="negative suffix"></ul>
<p><br />
As a suffix, first appears in texts from the late <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muromachi_period" class="extiw" title="w:Muromachi period">Muromachi period</a> as an eastern-<a href="/wiki/dialect" title="dialect">dialect</a> term.
</p><p>Various etymologies are proposed:
</p>
<ul><li>From ancient eastern-dialect negative ending <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AA%E3%81%B5#Japanese" title="なふ">なふ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nafu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> (perhaps through intermediate form <i>nafi</i>/<i>nahi</i>).
<ul><li>There is a sizable gap of time between the apparent disappearance of negative auxiliary suffix <i>nafu</i> in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heian_period" class="extiw" title="w:Heian period">Heian period</a> and the emergence of <i>nai</i> as a suffix in the late <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muromachi_period" class="extiw" title="w:Muromachi period">Muromachi period</a>, several centuries later.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-KDJ&#95;5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-KDJ-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Moreover, <i>nafu</i> conjugated as a verb, whereas <i>nai</i> conjugates as an adjective.</li></ul></li></ul>
<dl><dd><dl><dd>That said, both <i>nafu</i> and <i>nai</i> may derive ultimately from ancient <a href="/wiki/copula" title="copula">copula</a> or <a href="/wiki/stative" title="stative">stative</a> verb <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AC#Japanese" title="ぬ">ぬ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, with the negative sense possibly originating from the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E6%9C%AA%E7%84%B6%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="未然形"><ruby>未<rp>(</rp><rt>み</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>然<rp>(</rp><rt>ぜん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>mizenkei</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/irrealis" title="irrealis">irrealis</a> or <a href="/wiki/incomplete" title="incomplete">incomplete</a> form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> of the verb stem, to which these endings attach.</dd></dl></dd></dl>
<ul><li>From the adjective <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Etymology_2">ない</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> (etymology 2).
<ul><li>The <i>nai</i> auxiliary conjugates as a regular <i>-i</i> adjective just like the adjective <i>nai</i> in modern Japanese, but the patterns for the auxiliary were originally different from the adjective. In the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period" class="extiw" title="w:Edo period">Edo period</a>, the auxiliary conjugated irregularly, including <i>nanda</i> instead of modern <i>nakatta</i> (past), and <i>naikereba</i> instead of modern <i>nakereba</i> (conditional).<sup id="cite&#95;ref-KDJ&#95;5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-KDJ-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Derived directly from the negative suffix <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AC#Japanese" title="ぬ">ぬ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>-nu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.
<ul><li>The conjugation pattern for the negative suffix <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AC#Japanese" title="ぬ">ぬ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> was irregular, and not at all similar to the adjectival conjugation of modern <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">ない</strong></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.  Speculatively, the classical adjective-forming ending <i class="Jpan mention nowrap" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%8D#Japanese" title="き">-き</a></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">-ki</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> could have been appended to the irrealis form <i class="Jpan mention nowrap" lang="ja">-な</i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">-na</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> of the negative suffix <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AC#Japanese" title="ぬ">ぬ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.</li></ul></li></ul>
<div class="mw-heading mw-heading4"><h4 id="Pronunciation_3">Pronunciation</h4><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%AA%E3%81%84&amp;action=edit&amp;section=15" title="Edit section: Pronunciation"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>
<ul style="container-type: normal;"><li><a href="/wiki/Wiktionary:International_Phonetic_Alphabet" title="Wiktionary:International Phonetic Alphabet">IPA</a><sup>(<a href="/wiki/Appendix:Japanese_pronunciation" title="Appendix:Japanese pronunciation">key</a>)</sup>:&#32;<span class="IPA nowrap">[na̠i]</span></li></ul>
<ul><li>In Tokyo accent, the accent of the verb construction depends on the suffixed verb:<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>If the suffixed verb is accented, the accent falls on the final mora in the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E6%9C%AA%E7%84%B6%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="未然形"><ruby>未<rp>(</rp><rt>み</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>然<rp>(</rp><rt>ぜん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>mizenkei</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> stem. E.g. <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">つ<span style="border-top:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);position:relative;padding-top:1px;">く<span style="position:absolute;top:0;bottom:67%25;right:0%25;border-right:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);">​</span></span>る</span>&#160;<span class="Latn"><samp>[tsùkúꜜrù]</samp></span> (<a href="/wiki/%E4%B8%AD%E9%AB%98%E5%9E%8B" title="中高型">Nakadaka</a>) → <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">つ<span style="border-top:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);position:relative;padding-top:1px;">くら<span style="position:absolute;top:0;bottom:67%25;right:0%25;border-right:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);">​</span></span>ない</span>&#160;<span class="Latn"><samp>[tsùkúráꜜnàì]</samp></span> (<a href="/wiki/%E4%B8%AD%E9%AB%98%E5%9E%8B" title="中高型">Nakadaka</a>).</li>
<li>If the suffixed verb is non-accented, the result:
<ul><li>is non-accented if the suffix takes the following forms: <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">ない</strong></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない<a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A8#Japanese" title="と">と</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai to</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない<a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A4%E3%82%82%E3%82%8A#Japanese" title="つもり">つもり</a><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A0#Japanese" title="だ">だ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai tsumori da</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない<a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AF%E3%81%9A#Japanese" title="はず">はず</a><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A0#Japanese" title="だ">だ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai hazu da</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない<a href="/wiki/%E5%A0%B4%E5%90%88#Japanese" title="場合"><ruby>場<rp>(</rp><rt>ば</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>合<rp>(</rp><rt>あい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai baai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span></li>
<li>has an accent falling on the first mora of the suffix, viz. <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">な</span> <span class="IPA nowrap">[na]</span>, if the suffix takes the following forms: <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない<a href="/wiki/%E3%81%8B%E3%82%89#Japanese" title="から">から</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai kara</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない<a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7#Japanese" title="で">で</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren class="tr"><i>nai nara</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%AA%E3%81%8F%E3%81%A6&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="なくて (page does not exist)">なくて</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren <li>There are a few exceptions like <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E6%81%90%E3%82%8C%E5%85%A5%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="恐れ入る"><ruby>恐<rp>(</rp><rt>おそ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>れ<ruby>入<rp>(</rp><rt>い</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>る</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>osoreiru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>: <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">お<span style="border-top:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);position:relative;padding-top:1px;">そ<span style="position:absolute;top:0;bottom:67%25;right:0%25;border-right:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);">​</span></span>れいる</span>&#160;<span class="Latn"><samp>[òsóꜜrèìrù]</samp></span> (<a href="/wiki/%E4%B8%AD%E9%AB%98%E5%9E%8B" title="中高型">Nakadaka</a>) → <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">お<span style="border-top:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);position:relative;padding-top:1px;">そ<span style="position:absolute;top:0;bottom:67%25;right:0%25;border-right:1px solid var(--wikt-palette-black,black);">​</span></span>れいらない</span>&#160;<span class="Latn"><samp>[òsóꜜrèìrànàì]</samp></span> (<a href="/wiki/%E4%B8%AD%E9%AB%98%E5%9E%8B" title="中高型">Nakadaka</a>)</li></ul></li></ul>

<ul><li>Attaches to the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E6%9C%AA%E7%84%B6%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="未然形"><ruby>未<rp>(</rp><rt>み</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>然<rp>(</rp><rt>ぜん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>形<rp>(</rp><rt>けい</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>mizenkei</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/irrealis" title="irrealis">irrealis</a> or <a href="/wiki/incomplete" title="incomplete">incomplete</a> form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> of the verb. For <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E4%BA%94%E6%AE%B5%E6%B4%BB%E7%94%A8#Japanese" title="五段活用"><ruby>五<rp>(</rp><rt>ご</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>段<rp>(</rp><rt>だん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>活<rp>(</rp><rt>かつ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>用<rp>(</rp><rt>よう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>godan katsuyō</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/w/index.php?title=pentagrade&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="pentagrade (page does not exist)">pentagrade</a> conjugation</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span>, <span class="ann-pos">also known in English as “type 1”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> verbs, this is the stem form ending in <i>-a</i>.
<ul><li><span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">ない</strong></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>-nai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> does not attach to any form of the verb <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="ある">ある</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>aru</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">to <a href="/wiki/be" title="be">be</a>, to <a href="/wiki/exist" title="exist">exist</a></span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>. The negative form of <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="ある">ある</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>aru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> in the modern standard language is the adjective <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Etymology_2">ない</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, not <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">&#42;あらない</span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>*aranai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>. However, other negative suffixes do attach to <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="ある">ある</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>aru</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, as we see in forms such as <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%89%E3%81%9A#Japanese" title="あらず">あらず</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>arazu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%89%E3%81%AC#Japanese" title="あらぬ">あらぬ</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>aranu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%82%E3%82%89%E3%82%93&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="あらん (page does not exist)">あらん</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>aran</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%82%E3%82%89%E3%81%B8%E3%82%93#Japanese" title="あらへん">あらへん</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>arahen</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.</li></ul></li>
<li>This word is morphologically an <a href="/wiki/inflectional" title="inflectional">inflectional</a> <a href="/wiki/suffix" title="suffix">suffix</a>. It is classified as a <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E5%8A%A9%E5%8B%95%E8%A9%9E#Japanese" title="助動詞"><ruby>助<rp>(</rp><rt>じょ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>動<rp>(</rp><rt>どう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby><ruby>詞<rp>(</rp><rt>し</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>jodōshi</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/auxiliary_verb" title="auxiliary verb">auxiliary verb</a></span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> in traditional Japanese grammar.</li>
<li>Generally, <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%BE%E3%81%9B%E3%82%93#Japanese" title="ません">ません</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>masen</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%BE%E3%81%9B%E3%82%93%E3%81%A7%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="ませんでした (page does not exist)">ませんでした</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>masen deshita</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> (past) are the recommended formal negative endings, instead of the somewhat less formal <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">ない</strong><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7%E3%81%99#Japanese" title="です">です</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai desu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%AA%E3%81%8B%E3%81%A3%E3%81%9F#Japanese" title="なかった">なかった</a><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7%E3%81%99#Japanese" title="です">です</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nakatta desu</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>, which are considered incorrect by conservative speakers. The original incompatibility of <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない</span> and <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">です</span> may be explained by the origin of <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">ない</span> as a verb, <a href="/wiki/cf." title="cf.">cf.</a> the Etymology section.</li>
<li>The auxiliary <i>nai</i> and the adjective <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a class="mw-selflink-fragment" href="#Etymology_2"><ruby>無<rp>(</rp><rt>な</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>い</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>nai</i></span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> have mostly <a href="/wiki/converge" title="converge">converged</a> in modern usage.  One distinction that is still maintained by some speakers appears when attaching <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%9D%E3%81%86#Japanese" title="そう">そう</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>-sō</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/appear" title="appear">appears</a> like, <a href="/wiki/seem" title="seem">seems</a> like</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> or <span class="Jpan" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%99%E3%81%8E%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="すぎる">すぎる</a></span> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="tr"><i>-sugiru</i></span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss"><a href="/wiki/too" title="too">too</a>, <a href="/wiki/too_much" title="too much">too much</a>, in <a href="/wiki/excess" title="excess">excess</a></span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>:
<ul><li>Adjective <i>nai</i> + <i>sō</i> or <i>sugiru</i> → <i>na<b>sa</b>sō</i>, <i>na<b>sa</b>sugiru</i></li>
<li>Auxiliary <i>nai</i> + <i>sō</i> or <i>sugiru</i> → <i>nasō</i>, <i>nasugiru</i></li>
<li>The process of <a href="/wiki/convergence" title="convergence">convergence</a> is ongoing, and the <span class="Jpan" lang="ja">さ</span>-infix might be used by some speakers even with the auxiliary.  This could be considered as <a href="/wiki/Appendix:Glossary#proscribed" title="Appendix:Glossary">proscribed</a> in formal contexts, as <a href="/wiki/slang" title="slang">slang</a>, or as a grammatical error.</li></ul></li></ul>
