<div class="mw-heading mw-heading3"><h3 id="Etymology_1">Etymology 1</h3><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/w/index.php?title=%E3%81%99%E3%82%8B&amp;action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Etymology 1"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></div>

<p>From <span class="etyl"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Japanese" class="extiw" title="w:Old Japanese">Old Japanese</a></span> root verb <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%99#Japanese" title="す">す</a></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">su</span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">to do</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-KDJ2&#95;1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-KDJ2-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-DJR&#95;2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DJR-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Cognate with <span class="etyl"><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okinawan_language" class="extiw" title="w:Okinawan language">Okinawan</a></span> <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ryu"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%99%E3%82%93#Okinawan" title="すん">すん</a></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span lang="ryu-Latn" class="mention-tr tr Latn">sun</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.
</p><p>As with all verbs, during the Middle Japanese stage in the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamakura_period" class="extiw" title="w:Kamakura period">Kamakura</a> and <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muromachi_period" class="extiw" title="w:Muromachi period">Muromachi</a> periods, the <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E7%B5%82%E6%AD%A2%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="終止形">終止形</a></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">shūshikei</span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">terminal or sentence-ending form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> was gradually lost as the <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E9%80%A3%E4%BD%93%E5%BD%A2#Japanese" title="連体形">連体形</a></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">rentaikei</span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">attributive form</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> came to be used for both the attributive and terminal grammatical roles, realigning the conjugations.
</p>

<ul><li>The verb <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja">する</i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">suru</span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">to do</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> is seldom written in kanji (<i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E7%82%BA%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="為る">為る</a></i>).</li>
<li>It is common to use <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja">する</i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">suru</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> after certain nouns to indicate that the noun is being done; this is <i>highly</i> <a href="/wiki/productive" title="productive">productive</a>, meaning many nouns can be used as verbs in this way. Some examples are:

Verbal nouns that are not bound to the <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">する</strong></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">suru</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> can form potential forms by changing the <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">する</strong></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">suru</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> to <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7%E3%81%8D%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="できる">できる</a></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">dekiru</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> directly: <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><a href="/wiki/%E7%90%86%E8%A7%A3#Japanese" title="理解">理解</a><a href="/wiki/%E3%81%A7%E3%81%8D%E3%82%8B#Japanese" title="できる">できる</a></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">rikai dekiru</span>, <span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">“</span><span class="mention-gloss">to be able to understand</span><span class="mention-gloss-double-quote">”</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span>.</li>
<li>In headlines and enumeration of events, the ending <i class="Jpan mention" lang="ja"><strong class="selflink">する</strong></i> <span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">(</span><span class="mention-tr tr">suru</span><span class="mention-gloss-paren annotation-paren">)</span> can be dropped, leaving the verbal noun at the end of the sentence provided that it is not bound:
<dl><dd><span lang="ja" class="Jpan e-example"><ruby>実験<rp>(</rp><rt>じっけん</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>に<b><ruby>成功<rp>(</rp><rt>せいこう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></b> &#8212; <ruby>世界初<rp>(</rp><rt>せかいはつ</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></span><dl><dd><i><span class="e-transliteration tr">Jikken ni <b>seikō</b> &#8212; sekai-hatsu</span></i></dd><dd><span class="e-translation"><b>Successful</b> experiments &#8212; world first</span></dd></dl></dd>
<dd><span lang="ja" class="Jpan e-example"><ruby>空港<rp>(</rp><rt>くうこう</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby>にやっと<b><ruby>到着<rp>(</rp><rt>とうちゃく</rt><rp>)</rp></ruby></b>。</span><dl><dd><i><span class="e-transliteration tr">Kūkō ni yatto <b>tōchaku</b>.</span></i></dd><dd><span class="e-translation">Finally <b>at</b> the airport.</span></dd></dl></dd></dl></li>
