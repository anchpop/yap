Pronunciation

[edit]
  • IPA(key): /bæsˈtɛə(ɹ)/
  • Audio (Southern England):(file)

Proper noun

[edit]

Basse-Terre

  1. The capital city of the overseas department of Guadeloupe, France, located in the Lesser Antilles.
  2. An island comprising the western half of the overseas department of Guadeloupe.

French

[edit]

Etymology

[edit]

Compound of bas (“low”) +‎ terre (“land”).

Pronunciation

[edit]
  • IPA(key): /bas.tɛʁ/ ~ /bɑs.tɛʁ/
  • Audio (France (Saint-Maurice-de-Beynost)):(file)

Proper noun

[edit]

Basse-Terre f

  1. Basse-Terre (the capital city of the overseas department of Guadeloupe, France)
  2. Basse-Terre (an island comprising the western half of the overseas department of Guadeloupe)
