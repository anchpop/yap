Etymology

[edit]

Unadapted borrowing from Malay Bandar Seri Begawan. Literally: "Port/City of the Revered Ruler".

Pronunciation

[edit]
  • IPA(key): /ˌbɑːndəɹ sɪˌɹiː bɪˈɡaʊ.ən/
  • Audio (Southern England):(file)

Proper noun

[edit]

Bandar Seri Begawan

  1. The capital city of Brunei.
